Backups & recovery

Save a copy of every store, or restore a saved copy.

An all-store backup is a JSON file you keep on your computer. Restore adds copies of the saved stores; it never overwrites a store.

Store and account controls

A fresh backup downloads before clearing or deleting this store.

Clear all resets stock, bills, suppliers, purchases, expenses, payroll and receipt details but keeps this store and your login. Delete this store removes only this workspace.

Account deletion removes your login and every store. Deploy the included secure Supabase function first.
